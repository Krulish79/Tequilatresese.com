Our Story

Certified organic. Zero additives. Always.

For five generations the Tres Ese family has worked the red earth of Los Altos de Jalisco — with no herbicides, no synthetic fertilizers, no shortcuts. Each agave is grown organically and hand-selected at full maturity, then slowly transformed into tequila by fire, wood, copper, and time.

Nothing is added in the bottle. No sugar. No glycerin. No flavors. No colorants. Only what the plant, the earth, and the mountain sun quietly intended.

— Familia Tres Ese

The Process

Only agave, fire, wood,
copper and time.

Five steps, made by hand — the same way our great-great-grandfather made them.

01

Cultivation — hand-cut jimadores

Six to eight years of patient sun in the highlands. Every agave hand-selected at full maturity by jimadores who learned the trade from their fathers before them.

02

Slow roast — brick ovens

The piñas rest in traditional masonry ovens where low, patient heat wakes the natural sugars of the agave over days, not hours.

03

Natural fermentation — open wood vats

Only well water from 400 meters deep and the wild yeasts that live in the pores of the wood. Slower. Less predictable. The way tequila was always meant to be made.

04

Double distillation — copper stills

The copper polishes the flavors. The second pass refines the heart. A clean distillate that needs to mask nothing.

05

Bottled — by hand, numbered

Every bottle filled, corked, and labeled by hand. Small batches finished before the next begins. Every bottle numbered.
